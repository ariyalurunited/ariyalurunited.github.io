ONE TOWN. ONE TEAM. ONE UNITED SPIRIT.

Rooted in Ariyalur, united by football. We bring local players and supporters together on and off the pitch.

OUR STORY

Founded in 2021, Ariyalur United FC grew from a shared love of football into a club for our community.

OUR VISION

To create opportunities for local talent, strengthen football in Ariyalur and inspire the next generation.

OUR VALUES

Unity in every match. Discipline in every session. Respect for teammates, opponents and our community.
