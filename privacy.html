Privacy

This website is a static site with a browser-only demo for squad and formation editing. It does not connect to a database or send demo edits to a server.

Admin sign-in and browser storage

The demo login is client-side only and is not secure authentication. Demo credentials are visible in the website code. Squad and formation edits are saved in this browser's local storage and are not shared with other visitors, browsers, or devices. Clearing browser data removes those demo edits.

Do not enter sensitive personal information in player fields.

Contact forms

The contact form prepares a message in your device's email application. The message is sent only if you choose to send it from that application. The club's email provider then handles it under its own privacy terms.

External services

Links to Instagram, YouTube, and Google Maps open third-party services. Their privacy policies apply when you visit those services. This site does not embed their feeds.

Questions

For privacy questions, email ariyalurunited@gmail.com.
